Songbook button for Chrome

One click on any Ultimate Guitar chords page sends the song to your Drive › Songbook folder, ready for the glasses.

1. Put the button on your bookmarks bar
  1. If you can't see the bookmarks bar, press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this yellow button up onto the bookmarks bar:
    ➕ Songbook
2. Use it
  1. Open a song's Chords page on Ultimate Guitar (transpose it first if you like, and the button saves it as shown).
  2. Click ➕ Songbook on the bookmarks bar. A tab opens with the song filled in.
  3. Type the tempo (there's a "Look it up" link), check the capo, press Enter. Done.

The first time, that tab asks for your Songbook address (the one ending in ?k=…). Paste it once and it's remembered.
